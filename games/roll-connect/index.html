How to play
  • Players take turns rolling one six-sided die.
  • Connect the rolled number from your own side to the centre.
  • Each player has an independent set of numbers. Connecting 3 for one player does not affect the other player's 3.
  • If you roll a number you have already connected, your turn is skipped.
  • Lines can cross freely. The first player to connect all six numbers wins.
